Press START (or Space) to play. Select a track with its voice key, then click the step keys.

SHOGUN is the drum computer of the Martial Systems Jidai Collection: fourteen drum voices and two note voices, a sequencer of 1 to 32 steps per track, and patch jacks styled like BUSHIDO's and RONIN's. The sound is the SHOGUN engine itself, compiled for the browser. Press a voice key while stopped to hear that voice. Tracks: a voice's key selects its track, and its lamp shows which track is selected. In TRACK, LENGTH sets that track from 1 to 32 steps, SHUFFLE delays its odd steps (16 settings), SHIFT moves the whole track later (up to 30 ms) and MUTE silences it. The 16 step keys are a page: 1-16 and 17-32 switch pages, and steps past the track's length are dimmed. Click a step key to turn it on or off. The lamp under a step is its accent: off is soft, green is medium, red is loud; click it to change. CLEAR empties the selected track. Step edits: the last step key you clicked is the edit step (its number is in the EDIT screen). FLAM picks one of 16 flams or none, BEND sets a pitch bend on BD 1, BD 2, the snare and the toms, and NOTE and TIE set the note and the tie on the LEAD and BASS tracks. Patterns: click the PATTERN screen for the pattern list (the factory patterns, then your saved ones, up to 999), type to search, use the arrow keys or scroll, then click a row or press Enter. ◀ ▶ step through the list. SAVE writes over a saved pattern; on a factory pattern it asks for a name. COPY saves the pattern under a new name. Saved patterns keep their knobs and stay in this browser. Kits: KIT lists the sounds: loading one changes the knobs and keeps the steps, and its SAVE stores the knobs as a kit. Chain: + adds the loaded pattern to the chain, − removes the last (Shift-click clears it), and clicking the CHAIN screen turns it on: each pattern then plays one bar, in order. The chain is kept. Under each step: the ACC lamp shows the accent (dim green soft, green medium, red loud) and the line below shows the flam (F1 to F16) and the bend in semitones, or the note on LEAD and BASS (~ = tied). UNDO takes back the last knob move or key press (Ctrl+Z; Shift-click or Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes). SOLO lets only the selected track be heard (press it again to hear every track; a muted track stays silent). RANDOM rolls the selected voice's knobs inside their ranges. MIDI: notes 36 to 51 play the tracks in order, as in the plugin. To change one, press LEARN, pick a track, hit a pad; the next track is then selected. Press LEARN again to finish. Track: click the TRACK screen for the list of tracks, or use its ◀ ▶. Clock: TEMPO runs from 60 to 180 BPM, SCALE sets the steps per beat (32nd, 16th triplet, 16th, 8th triplet), BAR sets the bar length the STEP screen counts. INT lets the pattern fire the voices. EXT cuts the pattern off: each drum fires only from its TRIG jack, and the clock keeps counting. RST IN restarts the count, RUN IN starts or stops, and with CLK IN patched each pulse there is one step. On this page CLK OUT is the gate to patch: into a TRIG jack in EXT, or into RST IN, RUN IN or CLK IN. Outputs: the paired outs are taps before MASTER, and every voice stays in MAIN whether or not its pair is patched. MASTER scales MAIN only. The Monitor slider above sets how loud the page plays. Knobs: drag up or down (Shift = fine), scroll, double-click resets. Drag from a jack to a jack, drag a plug to move it, drop it on empty space to unplug, click a patched jack for its stack. Esc cancels.
